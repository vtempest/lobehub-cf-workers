# Personal Statistics and Activity Sharing

## Features

- **Personal statistics page.** A clear view of your activity patterns, from Agent usage to message volume.
- **Overview counts.** Agents created, topics started, messages sent, and total words generated.
- **Top lists.** Your most-used models, favorite Agents, and longest topics.
- **Activity heatmap.** A visual timeline of your usage over the past year, so you can see how it has grown.
- **Shareable activity card.** Generate a personal image summarizing your stats to post on social media or compare AI workflows with teammates.
- **Available in PgLite and Database modes.** Open your account menu and go to **Data Statistics** — the dashboard loads automatically.
