# Supporting TTS & STT Voice Conversations

## Features

- **Text-to-Speech.** Agents can read their responses aloud in clear, natural-sounding voices.
- **Speech-to-Text.** Dictate messages instead of typing them.
- **Hands-free interaction.** TTS and STT together are useful when you're multitasking, on the move, or simply prefer speaking to typing — and they help auditory learners, commuters away from a keyboard, and anyone who finds voice more accessible than text.
- **Per-Agent voices.** Each Agent can have its own voice, so a professional assistant can sound calm and measured while a creative collaborator sounds more expressive.
- **Curated voice library.** High-quality voices from OpenAI Audio and Microsoft Edge Speech cover a range of regions and preferences.
- **A complete communication loop.** Speak naturally, hear responses aloud, and keep context the way you would in a spoken conversation — plugins, multimodal support, and context management all work alongside voice mode.
