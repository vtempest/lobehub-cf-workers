# Authentication That Adapts to Your Stack

## Features

- **Two authentication paths.** LobeHub integrates both next-auth and Clerk, so you can start simple with social logins and upgrade to enterprise-grade controls later without rethinking your auth architecture.
- **Next-Auth for a fast start.** A straightforward authentication layer covering the essentials — OAuth from major providers, email login, and credential flows — with no separate user service to manage.
- **Quick social sign-in setup.** Connect providers like GitHub or Google in minutes.
- **Session management that just works.** Sessions are handled for you, with full control left over the sign-in UI and flow.
- **Privacy-friendly auth.** User data stays in your own infrastructure.
- **Clerk for enterprise identity.** A managed identity platform that goes past sign-in and scales with your product.
- **Multi-factor authentication.** Protect sensitive accounts and meet compliance requirements.
- **Built-in profile and account management.** User profile and account UI ships out of the box.
- **Login activity tracking.** Monitor sign-in activity in real time.
- **Scalable identity without ops overhead.** Identity infrastructure grows with usage instead of needing to be run.

## Improvements

- **next-auth v5 beta support.** Session handling is improved on the new major version.
- **Clearer OAuth errors.** Failed OAuth connections now explain what went wrong.

## Fixes

- **Custom sign-in pages no longer loop.** The redirect loop that hit custom sign-in pages is fixed.
