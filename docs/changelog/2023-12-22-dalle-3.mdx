# Support for Text-to-Image Generation

## Features

- **Text-to-image generation in chat.** [`DALL-E 3`](https://openai.com/dall-e-3), [`MidJourney`](https://www.midjourney.com/), and [`Pollinations`](https://pollinations.ai/) can be invoked by your Agent, so a description becomes an image inside the same conversation.
- **No tool switching.** Generating an AI image used to mean leaving the conversation, opening a separate tool, writing a prompt, waiting, and copying the result back. Now you describe what you want and the image appears in the chat.
- **Fast iteration.** Request adjustments, explore variations, and refine descriptions without a context switch — the conversation history keeps your creative direction, so you can reference earlier ideas and build on them.
- **Private, self-contained creation.** Prompts, iterations, and final images stay inside your existing conversation, with no separate accounts to manage or platforms to jump between.
- **Multiple generation options.** DALL-E 3 gives detailed, precise renders, MidJourney produces artistic and atmospheric results, and Pollinations offers fast, accessible generation — your Agent can help pick the right one for each task.
